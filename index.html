Algebraic Geometry

A moduli space of character sheaves (preprint; arXiv link forthcoming)
arXiv PDF
Abstract

We study character sheaves on a commutative connected algebraic group G: line bundles with flat connection (𝓛,∇) satisfying a multiplicativity isomorphism m*(𝓛,∇) ≃ (𝓛,∇) ⊠ (𝓛,∇), where m: G×G→G is the group law. We construct a group algebraic space G♭ whose points parametrize such character sheaves, and we analyze its geometry. The main technical ingredient is a study of extension sheaves on the de Rham space GdR. An appendix provides self-contained, elementary proofs of basic results on de Rham spaces that may be of independent interest.
